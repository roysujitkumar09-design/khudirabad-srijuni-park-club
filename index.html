About Our Club

Located at Srijani Park Road, Bhagabanpur, West Bengal, 2 No. Khudirabad Srijuni Park Club is a community organization fostering unity, cultural heritage, and youth sports across generations.

Through voluntary blood donation camps, social welfare drives, and traditional festivals like Durga Puja and Saraswati Puja, we strive to build a caring and vibrant neighborhood.
