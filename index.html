About Club

Welcome to 2 No. Khudirabad Srijuni Park Club, located at Srijani Park Road, Bhagabanpur, West Bengal. Our club is dedicated to promoting community unity, cultural heritage, sports activities, social welfare programs, blood donation camps, and festive celebrations. We strive to bring residents together and create a stronger, more vibrant community for all generations.
